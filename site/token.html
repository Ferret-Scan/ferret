    What holding it does

    At launch

    Vote

    Holders choose which fund gets added next. A vote is a free signature, weighted by balance.

    At launch

    Buyback

    The creator share of Pons trading fees is set to buy $FERRET off the market.

    Later

    Alerts

    Insider buys and fund changes sent to holders in Telegram as they are filed.

    Later

    Baskets

    A fund's on-chain book bought in one transaction, with a lower fee for holders.

    Bought-back tokens sit in the Pons buyback vault, which releases them over five years. They are not burned. The "later" items are not built and may change. The token gives no claim on any asset or revenue, and the site is free without it.
