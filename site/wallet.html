Wallet check

Paste any address. The ferret reads its stock token balances and tells you which fund it trades like. Nothing is signed and nothing is spent.

How the score works

  1. Balances

    The page asks Robinhood Chain for the balance of every stock token in the official registry.

  2. Prices

    Each balance is valued with its Chainlink feed, or with the last filing price where there is no feed.

  3. Overlap

    The weights are compared with the on-chain part of every fund book. 100% means an exact copy.
